Charleston, SC · The Field Guide

The Outdoor Bachelorette Party Guide to Charleston

Photo via Pexels

Bachelorette Adventures is a Charleston guide for brides who would rather paddle a tidal creek than sit through another bottomless brunch. Everything here is in or around Charleston, SC, organized so you can build a weekend that keeps the group outside and moving.

Use the contents below to jump to each section, then open the full guide for the details, outfitters and local spots worth booking.

01 · Water Sports

Charleston Water Sports for a Bachelorette Party

Paddle Shem Creek with dolphins, take a surf lesson at Folly Beach, sail Charleston Harbor at sunset on a tall ship, or rent a boat out of Isle of Palms for a sandbar day.

Charleston Tour Guides for an Outdoor Bachelorette

Walking tours of the Battery and South of Broad, ghost tours after dark, food tours through Upper King, carriage rides from the City Market and guided garden walks on the Ashley River.

03 · Bar Brawl

The Charleston Bar Brawl: A Bachelorette Bar Crawl

A bar crawl with a scoreboard. Split into teams, take on a challenge at every stop, and work from harbor-view rooftops to breweries to a final round on Upper King.

04 · Fitness

Bachelorette Fitness in Charleston

Run the Ravenel Bridge at sunrise, hold a beach bootcamp at Folly or Isle of Palms, book a private yoga or barre class, or climb the wall at James Island County Park.

Charleston Outdoor Adventures for the Bride Tribe

Ferry to Boneyard Beach on Bulls Island, catch sunset at the Morris Island Lighthouse, visit the Angel Oak, walk the boardwalks at Caw Caw and hike the Francis Marion.

06 · After the Adventure

Private Entertainment for the Last Night

After a weekend of paddling, hiking and touring, plenty of groups wrap things up with a party back at the rental. Michael At Your Place is a Charleston-based male entertainer who brings the show to your house, hotel or vacation rental. His show is $200 and runs up to 90 minutes, with payment on arrival by cash, Venmo or Cash App.

He covers Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach and Isle of Palms, works brunch or late-night parties, and last-minute dates are welcome.
