Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BacheloretteAdventures.com ("we," "us" or "the site") handles information when you visit. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use this site. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you visit.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and third-party analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site. These tools collect anonymous usage data like pages viewed and time on site. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, styling from the Bootstrap CDN and images hosted by Pexels. These providers may receive your IP address and browser information when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.

Links to Other Websites

This guide links to local businesses and outside websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies before sharing information with them. Any booking or payment you make happens directly with that business.

How We Use Information

Any information collected is used to operate and improve the site, understand which content is useful, and keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults planning bachelorette celebrations and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
