The Charleston Bar Brawl: A Bachelorette Bar Crawl

By Bachelorette AdventuresUpdated November 20264 Min. Read
Photo via Pexels

The Bar Brawl is a bar crawl with a scoreboard. Split the bride tribe into teams, give every stop a challenge, and crown a winner at the last bar.

How the Bar Brawl Works

Pick four or five stops within walking distance, divide the group into two or three teams, and assign a challenge at each bar: best toast to the bride, a photo scavenger hunt, trivia about the couple, or a game of darts or cornhole wherever there is a board. Keep score on a phone and give the winning team bragging rights or the first round at the final stop. It works best as a walking route so nobody needs to drive.

Start on a Rooftop

Charleston's rooftops make a strong first round. The Rooftop at The Vendue looks over the harbor and Waterfront Park, and the Pavilion Bar at Market Pavilion Hotel has a pool deck and skyline views a few blocks away. Arrive before sunset for the best light and an easier time getting a group table.

Brewery and Distillery Rounds

A daytime Bar Brawl fits the outdoor theme better than a late night. Revelry Brewing has a rooftop deck on the upper peninsula, and Edmund's Oast is close by with a big beer list and a patio. In North Charleston, Holy City Brewing and Firefly Distillery have outdoor space, lawn games and room for a large group.

Finish on Upper King

Upper King Street is where the crawl ends up after dark, with bars, cocktail lounges and dance spots packed into a few walkable blocks. Save the final scoreboard reveal for here and let the winning team pick the last round.

Keep It Fun

Pace the drinks, eat between stops, and keep water in the mix, especially in summer. Book a rideshare or a party bus for the trip back to the rental rather than walking a tired group across town.
